Computational Structural Biology Lab · IBG, Izmir

How biomolecular interfaces form, persist and change

We combine AlphaFold-based sampling, molecular dynamics and integrative modelling to assess the accuracy of predicted assemblies, resolve the interface contacts that determine their binding specificity, and extract conformational dynamics from sequence-derived information such as AlphaFold distograms.

PublicationsGitHub

Research

Our research moves from static to dynamic views of biomolecular interfaces: we test how reliably they can be predicted, map how mutations rewire them, resolve which persistent contacts govern binding, and ask how much of this behaviour is encoded in sequence.

1Benchmarking predictors

AlphaFold-based pipelines model most assemblies well, but not all. Through CASP, CAPRI and dedicated benchmarks, we test how far structure and affinity predictors can be trusted, and build leaner sampling and scoring protocols.

MinnieFoldTROPICCASP14CASP15Affinity prediction benchmarks

2Interface change

A static interface already shows which positions matter. We map how mutations, disease variants and isoforms strengthen, weaken or abolish interfaces, and use these maps for functional characterisation or to redesign binders.

PROT-ONDisease variants and isoforms

3Interface dynamics

Many interface contacts are transient, and the ones that persist govern binding. We quantify contact persistence from simulations and compare related complexes to find the dynamic networks behind partner choice and function.

DynaPINDynaBenchDNMT3A/BSox–nucleosomeAK2–AIFM1Axl–Gas6

4Dynamics in sequence

What predictors learn from sequence also reflects flexibility. We extract this signal from AlphaFold distograms and AlphaFold-based sampling to predict interface motions and ensembles directly from sequence, without long simulations.

Software

Open-source tools from the lab, with code released on GitHub.

  • PROT-ON

    Structure-based detection of designer mutations at protein–protein interfaces. PROT-ON scans every interface position with EvoEF1 or FoldX (the web server runs EvoEF1), flags outlier mutations with an interquartile-range threshold, and filters them for stability and, optionally, evolutionary likelihood (PSSM) to propose mutations that strengthen or abolish binding. A web run takes under 10 minutes per partner. Test cases include MDM2–p53, ACE2–spike and MCL1–NOXA. Web serverUsage statisticsRepositoryPaper

    Try it in a HADDOCK tutorial

    PROT-ON is part of the official HADDOCK antibody–antigen tutorials, where it scans the docked interface for mutations that improve binding and helps identify an optimal binder sequence.

    • HADDOCK3 antibody–antigen tutorialPROT-ON with HADDOCK3 scoring
    • HADDOCK2.4 antibody–antigen tutorialInterface energetics with PROT-ON

  • DynaPIN

    Dynamic interface fingerprints from MD trajectories or structure ensembles: quality control, residue properties and interaction networks in one command.Repository

  • MinnieFold

    A 50-fold reduced version of massive AlphaFold2 sampling with custom ranking, for antibody–antigen and other complexes with shallow alignments.Repository

  • DistoFlex

    Reads flexibility from AlphaFold2 and AlphaFold3 distograms by locating multimodal residue-pair distance distributions.Repository

Principal Investigator

Ezgi Karaca

ez-ghee kah-rah-jah

Group Leader, Izmir Biomedicine and Genome Center · Associate Professor and Head of the Molecular Biology and Genetics Program, Dokuz Eylül University

Ezgi trained in chemical engineering at Boğaziçi University and did her PhD with Alexandre Bonvin at Utrecht University, where she became a long-time contributor to the HADDOCK integrative modelling platform. As a Humboldt postdoctoral fellow at EMBL Heidelberg she worked on integrative structure determination of molecular machines, before starting her group at IBG in 2017 with a Humboldt return fellowship.

She was an assessor of protein assemblies in CASP14 and CASP15, received the Science Academy Young Scientist Award (BAGEP) in 2020, and is the BioExcel Ambassador for Türkiye.
